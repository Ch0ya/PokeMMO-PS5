Privacy

What PokeMMO accesses, and what it does with it. Last updated 27 October 2026.

In short: the title has no servers of its own and collects nothing. Everything it keeps stays on your PS5. It talks to PokeMMO's servers only to download/update the client and to play the game.

What the title stores

WhereWhatWhy
The title's storage on the consoleThe downloaded PokeMMO client, its caches, each PS5 profile's PokeMMO settings (including PokeMMO's remembered login if you chose "remember me"), the start-up log and the graphics driver log So the game starts without downloading again, keeps your settings, and can show what happened in Settings > Log
The title itself (read-only)The game ROMs supplied when the title was built, under assets/roms So the client has its game files from the first start

Nothing here is sent to the project or its developers.

Diagnostic log

The loader writes a start-up log to its own storage and shows it in Settings > Log; the previous start's log and the graphics driver's log (which also receives the client's console output) are shown there too. The loader also broadcasts the same lines on the local network over UDP port 18194, which is useful when developing; a build can additionally name one PC to send them to. The logs are not uploaded to any server. They can contain the console's local IP address, the PokeMMO revision and file paths on the console.

Everything else the title connects to

WhereWhy
PokeMMO's download serverTo download and update the official PokeMMO client
PokeMMO's game serversThe game itself: your account and play are between you and PokeMMO, under their terms
GitHub's release APITo check whether a newer PokeMMO release exists (it only reads the version tag)

There is no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Removing your data

  • Delete the title on the PS5 to remove everything it keeps there.
  • The ROMs are part of the installed title, so deleting the title removes them too.
  • Your PokeMMO account and characters stay on PokeMMO's servers; deleting the title does not delete them.
